Three independent controls:
Group by — Section · Type · Color · CMC — determines how cards are bucketed into sections
View — List · Art · Art+Name — applies to any grouping (art grid shows card images instead of rows)
Sort — Name · Price ↓ · Price ↑ · CMC ↓ — sorts cards within each group

All 3 are independent and composable. You can group by Color, view as Art, and sort by Price descending — any combo works. Lands always appear last regardless of grouping.
