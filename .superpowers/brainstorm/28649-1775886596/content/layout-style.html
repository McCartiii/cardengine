Player Panel Layout

How should player panels be arranged on the phone screen?

A

Face-to-Face (current mobile)

Top player(s) rotated 180° so each player reads from their side of the table. Phone lays flat between players. Best for 2-player.

B

All Upright

All panels face the phone holder. One person operates the app for everyone. Better for 3-4 player pods where everyone can't reach.

C

Adaptive

2 players = face-to-face (rotated). 3-4 players = all upright (one operator). Automatically picks the best layout for the player count.
