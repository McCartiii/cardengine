Three approaches to a premium, distinctive design

All preserve the card-hover lift and shimmer. Pick the direction.

RARE
MYTHIC

A — Obsidian TCG

Dark as default. Deep black surfaces, gold glows, rarity color halos on cards. Like a premium card vault — nothing in the market looks like this.

Trade-off: dark-first means extra care for light mode.

RARE
MYTHIC

B — Parchment Elevated

Warm parchment texture as the actual background, amber-gold borders, metallic rarity badges. Feels like a beautifully designed physical binder app.

Trade-off: texture can feel heavy if overdone.

RARE
MYTHIC

C — Adaptive Depth

Light mode gets deep layering and color-identity tints on cards (blue card = blue glow, red/black = warm orange). Dark mode becomes truly premium. Same system, two faces.

Trade-off: most complex to implement consistently.
